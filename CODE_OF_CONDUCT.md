# Code of Conduct

MBC WalkWith contributors are expected to communicate with patience, honesty,
and respect. Welcome questions, critique ideas rather than people, protect the
privacy of church members and visitors, and avoid harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized conduct, or publication of another person's private data.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct harms people, the project, or its community. Report conduct issues
privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter and
others affected.
